## Overview

Tripora is a smart travel app designed to simplify trip planning and bookings through an intuitive, mobile-first interface.

## What I did

- Developed a unified platform for multi-mode travel bookings
- Introduced visual seat selection and dynamic pricing
- Improved usability with custom filters and simpler navigation
- Backed design decisions with user research and competitor insights
