  - type: text
    body: >
      Picture an investor deciding where to put their money. The usual routine is five
      different apps for prices, news and calculators, plus a stock tip in their inbox
      that leaves them second-guessing every decision.


      Active traders have the same problem at a faster pace: data scattered across
      tools, insights that arrive late, and not enough clarity for decisions that carry
      real money.


      **TradeRake is a stock-market intelligence platform built to fix that** — one place
      where investors get real-time market intelligence, traders get actionable insight,
      and everyone gets a clear, transparent view of the market.


      I designed it as a live project at Baron Tech Labs: the brand and marketing
      website first, then the product behind it.
  - type: callout
    label: The goal
    body: >

      and trading feel smarter and faster, without needing five tools to do it.

  - type: heading
    text: Blogs and tools
  - type: text
    body: >
      Both libraries were designed to grow, so they share one pattern: category tabs and
      search to jump straight to what you need, and a consistent card that makes a long
      list easy to scan.


      **Blogs.** Tabs for All, Stock, Wealth and Finance. Each card carries a category
      tag, date, title and "Read article". The mix — psychology, beginner basics, market
      fundamentals — supports readers at every stage of their investing journey.


      **Tools.** Tabs for Investment, Trading and Personal Finance. Each card has a
      category tag, icon, name and a one-line description, so a wide range of
      calculators can be compared at a glance.


      **CMS-driven.** Articles and calculators are both managed through a CMS, so the team
      could publish a new article or add a new tool without design or development
      support.

  - type: heading
    text: What I learned
  - type: text
    body: >
      **Design for the stage the product is at.** A pre-launch site cannot sell features
      people cannot use yet. Showing the product working, then asking for a small
      commitment, did more than a long feature list would have.


      **Structure is what makes dense data readable.** One repeated pattern with a
      distinct accent per market let six very different asset classes sit on one page
      without blurring together.


      **Build for the people who keep the site alive.** Making blogs and tools
      CMS-driven meant the site could keep growing after the design work was done.


      **Projects can end without warning.** Phase 2 was 90% designed when the company
      closed. Documenting as I went is the reason this work can still be shown.
